Every sound in the short is generated by `film/banana-karma/audio.js` when you render. There are no recorded samples and no third-party audio files.

Footsteps are not in the mix. A step that landed off the gait would fight the bass, and the swagger already reads in the picture.

Picture assets (the office worker, the phone, the coffee cup, the banana peel, the konbini, the kissaten, the vending machine, the ginkgo, the sidewalk) are the procedural models already in this repository. No new character or world models were added. The splat mark on the pavement is staging built for this short. The coffee cup is parented beside the worker because a person can hold only one hand item; the phone uses that slot. The stock street lamp was left out: from these camera heights it read as a black scaffold rather than a lamp.
