# Last Bus — audio credits

Every sound in the short is generated by `film/last-bus/audio.js` when you render. There are no recorded samples and no third-party audio files.

| Element | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rain bed (filtered noise and drop ticks) | synthesized in-repo | — | synthesized in-repo |
| Bus approach, pass-by and doppler whoosh | synthesized in-repo | — | synthesized in-repo |
| Puddle splash | synthesized in-repo | — | synthesized in-repo |
| Piano line (damped sine partials, A minor, written for this short) | synthesized in-repo | — | synthesized in-repo |

Picture assets (Grandpa, the kitten, the bus stop, the street lamp, the city bus, the town) are the procedural models already in this repository. No new character or world models were added. The puddle, the headlight and taillight cones, and the splash shapes are staging built for this short.
